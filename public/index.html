How streaks work
  • Sync on 7 days in a row: +5 bonus credits.
  • 30 days in a row: +20 bonus credits and 1 streak freeze.
  • 90 days in a row: +50 bonus credits and 3 streak freezes.
  • A streak freeze keeps your streak if you miss a day.
  • Bonus credits stay valid while your plan is active.
