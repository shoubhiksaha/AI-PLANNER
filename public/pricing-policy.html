Pricing Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Overview

AI Planner ("we", "us", "our") operates on a freemium model. We offer a free tier with limited functionality alongside paid subscription plans (Standard and Pro) and one-time consumable top-ups (Booster Packs) for users requiring higher usage limits and advanced features like batch uploads and custom prompts.

2. Paid Access Plans

We offer Standard and Pro access for 30 days, 90 days, or 12 months. Each purchase is paid in advance as a one-time Cashfree order. Plans do not renew or auto-debit automatically; you choose whether to purchase access again after it expires.

  • Standard Plan: Includes 100 Sync Credits per month, multi-page uploads, and access to standard AI models (e.g., Gemini 2.0 Flash).
  • Pro Plan: Includes 250 Sync Credits per month, premium LLM routing, custom AI prompts, and advanced batch uploads (up to 5 pages).

Annual plans are billed once for 12 months of access. Sprint plans are billed once for 90 days of access.

3. Credit System & Booster Packs

Usage of the AI Planner features consumes "Sync Credits".

  • Included tier credits refresh every 30 days while a paid access period is active. Unused tier credits do not roll over to the next 30-day period.
  • Booster Packs: Users may purchase one-time Booster Packs (e.g., 50 credits). Booster pack credits do not expire as long as your account remains active and are consumed only after your recurring monthly credits are exhausted.

4. Currency and Taxes

All prices are listed in Indian Rupees (INR) unless otherwise stated. Prices displayed on our website are inclusive of all applicable taxes (such as GST) as required by Indian law. The final checkout amount shown by our payment gateway represents the total cost to you.

5. Payment Processing

All payments are securely processed through our RBI-compliant payment gateway partner (Cashfree). We do not store your payment credentials (credit/debit card numbers, UPI handles) on our servers. For RBI compliance during recurring payment setup, we may collect a phone number which is securely passed directly to the gateway and explicitly not stored in our databases.

6. Price Changes

We reserve the right to modify pricing for future purchases. A price change does not shorten or otherwise alter an access period that has already been paid for.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Pricing Policy, please contact our billing support team at support@aiplanner.app.
