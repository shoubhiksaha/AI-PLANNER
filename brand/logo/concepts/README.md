# Concepts — AI Planner

## Strategy (step 3)

- **Logo type: combination mark** (symbol + "AI Planner" wordmark), with a symbol that must also work alone, because the most frequent contact is the phone home screen and the browser tab. The name is generic ("AI" + "Planner"), so recognition has to come from the symbol, not the words. (Brief: Identity, Where they meet the logo; principles §9, §11.)
- **Conventions**
  - Follow: one glyph in a **rounded-square tile** for the app icon. Every competitor's app icon does this; breaking it on a home screen only costs fluency.
  - Break: **blue** (Any.do, Google Tasks, Microsoft To Do, Lens are all blue) → ADT's aqua on deep black, which nobody in the lineup uses.
  - Break: the **plain tick** (Any.do, Google Tasks, To Do, and Todoist's stacked ticks: 4 of 9). A tick can only appear if its silhouette is changed.
  - Avoid: the calendar page with a date (Google Calendar owns it) and any destination's look.
- **Personality → form:** calm, practical, personal, quietly modern → mostly squared geometry with **one deliberate curve** standing for the hand. Space Grotesk 700 wordmark. Aqua on deep black; deep black on light. All three say "precise, with a human touch".
- **The one hook:** *a handwritten stroke that straightens into order*. One band, the same weight as ADT's band, that starts human (curved) and ends digital (squared). It echoes ADT's "analog in, digital out" without reusing ADT's waveform.

## Eight routes (one line each)

1. **Name literal:** a ring-bound planner, spiral coils down the left edge. Literal, and every planner shop uses it.
2. **Name hidden:** "P" for Planner whose bowl is a folded page corner; "AI" as two strokes, i.e. a letter in a shape (trap).
3. **What the customer gets:** a finished day, everything ticked, a calm empty slate. Leads to the tick (cliché) unless subverted.
4. **How it works:** a hand-drawn loop is read and comes out as a straight, squared line; the photo turns writing into structure.
5. **Metaphor:** a bookmark ribbon marking *today* whose tail folds into a tick; a stitch joining paper to screen.
6. **Category symbol, subverted:** the tick drawn the way a pen would (a loop), finishing as a right-angled digital tick; a page whose dog-ear fold makes the tick in negative space.
7. **Pure form:** three ruled lines, wavy at the top and settling to straight at the bottom, like scribbles being straightened.
8. **Family / story:** ADT's band and its square step reused as the page's tab; India-first, but nothing literal fits.

Negative-space flip tried on each: only route 6 (page fold) carries the idea in the gap.

## Finalists (sketch format)

```
c0 Control: "Planner page + tick"
Form:    rounded-square deep-black tile; a white notebook page with two binding
         holes along its top edge; an aqua tick on the page
Meaning: a planner, done
Routes:  1 + 3 (the obvious answer)
Type:    —
Color:   black tile, white page, aqua tick; mono = page outline + tick
Risk:    reads as "notes app"; generic by design; the bar to beat
```

```
c1 "Loop to tick"
Form:    one band (ADT weight): it enters at the left as a small handwritten
         loop, like a cursive l, then drops into the short arm of a tick with a
         squared (right-angle) bottom corner and runs up into the long arm,
         ending square
Meaning: your handwriting comes out as a finished task
Routes:  4 + 6 (the tick, subverted with a pen loop)
Type:    Space Grotesk 700 "AI Planner", aqua tick inside black tile
Color:   aqua on deep black; deep black on light
Risk:    the loop may read as a ribbon or a cursive "e"; a tick is still the
         category cliché, so the loop must be large enough to change the silhouette
```

```
c2 "Folded page"
Form:    a page (rounded square outline band); its bottom-right corner is
         folded inward as a solid triangle-ish flap; the gap between the flap
         and the page edge draws a tick in negative space
Meaning: a paper page, marked done
Routes:  6 + negative-space flip
Type:    Space Grotesk 700
Color:   aqua page on deep black
Risk:    reads as a generic document icon; the negative-space tick may close up
         at 16 px
```

```
c3 "Settling lines"
Form:    three horizontal bands of equal weight: the top is a full wave, the
         middle is half wave then straight, the bottom is straight with squared
         ends, like ruled lines being straightened
Meaning: messy notes settle into order
Routes:  7 + 4 (abstract)
Type:    Space Grotesk 700
Color:   aqua on deep black
Risk:    may read as water, a menu (hamburger) icon, or audio, like ADT's wave did
```

```
c4 "P that ticks"  (owner's P idea; the one lettermark allowed)
Form:    a P built from the band: square stem, rounded bowl; the bowl does not
         close back into the stem but ends in a short squared tick
Meaning: Planner, done
Routes:  2 + 3 (owner's /logo "P" exploration)
Type:    Space Grotesk 700
Color:   aqua on deep black
Risk:    a letter in a tile (the trap); the tick terminal may vanish at 16 px
```

Range rule: one lettermark (c4), meaning-derived (c1, c2), abstract (c3). No cliché-list item as the main idea (the tick is subverted in c1 and c2; the control uses it plainly by design).

## Round 2 — ADT family (owner, 2026-10-21: "a showcase frontline app made by ADT … minimise distraction")

Strategy change: AI Planner is not its own brand, so the mark joins the ADT family.
